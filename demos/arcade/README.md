Public link, no account needed: https://jhm-hub.github.io/scam-wheel/demos/arcade/

Choose a scam or Surprise me, then Spin. Play random round runs live random results; Play showcase is a clearly labelled scripted demonstration. Low/high stakes, amounts, base spins, wheel colours and sound are editable and save in the browser. Settings can also be saved to a file.

Editable source, full rules, provenance and checks: ../../arcade/README.md in the repository (the source lives at /arcade).

The page is self-contained, with a transparent companion view for the same browser/profile. It is a separate collection and leaves the existing production homepage unchanged. Nunito's OFL is included here and in the page.
